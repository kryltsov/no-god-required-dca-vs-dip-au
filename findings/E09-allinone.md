# E09: the delay test on other funds - Diversified all-in-one ETFs

Generated by `npx tsx src/run-experiment.ts funds-allinone`. Realistic model (README, "The model"): as-traded prices, whole units, $3 brokerage per buy with a smallest trade of $300 (brokerage at most 1% of a trade), dividends paid as cash on their pay dates into the same pot as contributions, cash carried forward below the minimum, parked cash earning the cash ETF's total return with no brokerage on parking. No tax or franking. "Gain" is final value (holdings at market plus all cash) minus contributions, after brokerage. Funds that hold several index funds in a fixed mix and rebalance it (VDHG, VDGR, VDBA, DHHF). They are managed to a set formula, not by someone picking stocks. Each fund on its own, $500 a month, realistic model, idle cash in AAA. Ratio = final value against DCA on the same fund over the same dates (1.000 = tie). "Full" is one run from the fund's first contribution to 2025-11-28. "5-year runs" are every five-year stretch starting in each month (medians; the count of runs is shown, and neighbouring runs overlap almost completely).
