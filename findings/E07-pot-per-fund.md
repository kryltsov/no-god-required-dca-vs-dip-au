# E07: a pot per fund, against DCA run the same way

Generated by `npx tsx src/run-experiment.ts sleeves`. Realistic model (README, "The model"): as-traded prices, whole units, $3 brokerage per buy with a smallest trade of $300 (brokerage at most 1% of a trade), dividends paid as cash on their pay dates into the same pot as contributions, cash carried forward below the minimum, parked cash earning the cash ETF's total return with no brokerage on parking. No tax or franking. "Gain" is final value (holdings at market plus all cash) minus contributions, after brokerage. Weights as in E02 (Stockspot Topaz). The baseline row is **DCA with a pot per fund**: each fund receives its share of every contribution and buys when that fund's pot clears $300. It holds the same money per fund as the dip-buyers, so any gap between them is timing, not a different mix. In E02 the baseline is one-pot DCA, which ends with a different mix.
