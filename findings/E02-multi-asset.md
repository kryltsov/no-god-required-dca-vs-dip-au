# Stockspot Topaz portfolio (VAS 47.3%, IOO 9.8%, IEM 20.9%, IAF 9.7%, GOLD 12.3%): DCA vs buying the dip

Generated by `npx tsx src/run-experiment.ts multi`. Realistic model (README, "The model"): as-traded prices, whole units, $3 brokerage per buy with a smallest trade of $300 (brokerage at most 1% of a trade), dividends paid as cash on their pay dates into the same pot as contributions, cash carried forward below the minimum, parked cash earning the cash ETF's total return with no brokerage on parking. No tax or franking. "Gain" is final value (holdings at market plus all cash) minus contributions, after brokerage. $500 a month split by these weights, first contribution 2013-01-01, data to 2025-11-28. Idle cash is parked in AAA. Signals fire on the first day of a dip and are acted on one trading day later. Buying uses "smart" allocation: each slice goes to the holding furthest under target. The shared calendar is VAS's trading days; other funds are carried forward on days they did not trade. IAF and AAA only list from March 2012, which sets the start. Windows overlap heavily.
