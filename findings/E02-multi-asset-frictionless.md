# Stockspot Topaz portfolio (VAS 47.3%, IOO 9.8%, IEM 20.9%, IAF 9.7%, GOLD 12.3%): DCA vs buying the dip

> **Frictionless comparison model, not the headline.** Generated by `npx tsx src/run-experiment.ts multi --frictionless`. Total-return prices: dividends reinvested instantly and free, fractional units, no brokerage, no tax. Kept to show how much the realistic model (README, "The model") changes the answer.

$500 a month split by these weights, first contribution 2013-01-01, data to 2025-11-28. Idle cash is parked in AAA. Signals fire on the first day of a dip and are acted on one trading day later. Buying uses "smart" allocation: each slice goes to the holding furthest under target. The shared calendar is VAS's trading days; other funds are carried forward on days they did not trade. IAF and AAA only list from March 2012, which sets the start. Windows overlap heavily.
