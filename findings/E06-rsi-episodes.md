# E06: every RSI(14) < 30 episode on VAS

Generated by `npx tsx src/run-experiment.ts rsi-episodes`. Realistic model (README, "The model"): as-traded prices, whole units, $3 brokerage per buy with a smallest trade of $300 (brokerage at most 1% of a trade), dividends paid as cash on their pay dates into the same pot as contributions, cash carried forward below the minimum, parked cash earning the cash ETF's total return with no brokerage on parking. No tax or franking. "Gain" is final value (holdings at market plus all cash) minus contributions, after brokerage. Prices are VAS's total-return (adjusted) close. "22 days later" is 22 trading days after the next-day price, the delay used in E03. Of 23 episodes, the price was higher 22 days later on 15 and lower on 8. The three episodes in February-March 2020 are the crash.
