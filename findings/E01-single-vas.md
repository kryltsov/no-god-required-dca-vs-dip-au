# VAS alone: DCA vs buying the dip

Generated by `npx tsx src/run-experiment.ts single-vas`. Realistic model (README, "The model"): as-traded prices, whole units, $3 brokerage per buy with a smallest trade of $300 (brokerage at most 1% of a trade), dividends paid as cash on their pay dates into the same pot as contributions, cash carried forward below the minimum, parked cash earning the cash ETF's total return with no brokerage on parking. No tax or franking. "Gain" is final value (holdings at market plus all cash) minus contributions, after brokerage. $500 on the first trading day of each month, first contribution 2013-01-01, data to 2025-11-28. Buy executes one trading day after the signal. "Median vs DCA" is strategy final value divided by DCA final value (1.000 = tie). Windows overlap heavily, so "Beats DCA" counts are far from independent evidence.
